# Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis using Excel

## 📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the **Sample Superstore dataset** using Microsoft Excel to understand sales, profit, customers, products, regions, discounts, and time-based performance.

The project includes data cleaning, exploratory analysis, PivotTables, charts, and an interactive dashboard to identify important business trends and insights.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Understand overall sales and profit performance
- Analyze sales across different regions and categories
- Identify profitable and loss-making products
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior
- Study the impact of discounts on profitability
- Identify monthly and yearly sales trends
- Create an interactive Excel dashboard
- Generate business insights and recommendations

## 💡 Key Insights

- **Total Sales:** ₹22.97 Lakhs
- **Total Profit:** ₹2.86 Lakhs
- **Total Quantity:** 37,873
- **Total Orders:** 9,994
- **Technology** generated the highest category sales.
- **West** generated the highest regional sales and profit.
- **Consumer** was the largest and most profitable customer segment.
- **November** recorded the highest monthly sales.
- **Furniture** had the lowest profit margin among the three categories.
- Higher discounts showed a **weak negative relationship with profit**, with a correlation of approximately **-0.2195**.
- **155 customers** had negative total profit.
- Some high-sales products and orders generated relatively low or negative profit and therefore require further business review.

## 📌 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- Review discount levels, especially where they are associated with low profitability.
- Review loss-making products and high-sales, low-profit products.
- Focus on profitable categories while monitoring margins.
- Analyze regional performance regularly.
- Improve profitability in areas with high discounts and lower margins.
- Monitor customer profitability along with sales volume.
- Track shipping performance and delivery times regularly.
